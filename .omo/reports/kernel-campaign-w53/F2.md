## Files changed

- `packages/agent/src/session-lifecycle/inspect.ts`
  - Finding 3: the descendant visit budget (`descendants`, formerly `nodes`)
    no longer counts the mandatory root visit; it is decremented only when a
    child is actually visited. With `limit: 1` and an empty root page, one
    child now advances per continuation, and every advertised
    `nextChildrenCursor` either advances past the last visited child or
    terminates (null on an empty child page). The doc comment on
    `inspectSession` states the new budget contract.
  - Finding 4: `inspectActions` now receives a `resolveAction` callback
    (`reader.actionById`) and resolves out-of-page turn ancestry by walking
    only the needed ancestor chain through the indexed by-id read, memoizing
    the trail into the page's `turns` map. In-page attribution is unchanged
    (map hit, zero ledger reads). No loop-to-head scan was restored: reads are
    one indexed lookup per previously unseen ancestor actually referenced by
    the slice.
- `packages/agent/test/session-inspection.test.ts`
  - New describe "bounded inspection pages keep advancing and keep causal
    attribution (review F3/F4)" with two regression tests (details below).
- `packages/ledger` CatalogStore.childSessionsPage: NOT touched. No bounded
  index read was required — `kernel.actionById` (an existing indexed read on
  the session store) already provides the bounded ancestor resolution, and the
  existing `childSessionsPage(parentId, afterId, limit)` index suffices for
  the F3 continuation semantics.

## Tests added (both fail before the fix, pass after; no sleeps, pure sync reads over a real in-memory ledger)

1. "limits 1 and 2 advance children from an empty root page and every
   continuation advances or terminates" — materializes root + child-a +
   child-b (catalog-indexed), inspects at head with `limit: 1` three times
   (child-a, then child-b, then termination with null cursor) and with
   `limit: 2` (both children, then termination). Pre-fix failure: limit 1
   returned no children and `nextChildrenCursor: ""` forever.
2. "a page boundary between a turn and its tool intent preserves the tool's
   turn attribution" — ledger with configure@1, turn-1@2, tool-1@3; asserts
   the complete inspection and the `{depth:0, cursor:2, limit:1}` page both
   give `tool-1.turnId === "turn-1"` with equal revision and digest. Pre-fix
   failure: paged `turnId` was `null`.
