# Bets and Kill Criteria

This project's founding principle is *evidence over self-report*. This document applies that principle to the project itself: the strongest standing criticisms of the design, the falsifiable bets the impact rests on, and the pre-committed criteria under which claims get downgraded. Written 2026-06-11 after a full adversarial review; extended 2026-07-03 with the philosophy-v2 bets; revisit at each checkpoint below.

## 1. Standing criticisms (the indictment)

Kept here so they cannot be quietly forgotten. None are refuted yet — they are answered only by the measurements in §2–3.

1. **An OS with one app is not an OS.** The dispatch "syscall table" currently has exactly one caller — our own Resident. Until third parties target the ABI, "OS" is branding. (→ T1, #216)
2. **The kernel is a convention, not a privilege boundary.** Kernel and userland share one Bun process; only three guarantees are mechanism-enforced today, and the Resident still holds direct MCP tools — a gate with a side door. (→ T2, #218/#221)
3. **`await world` is less novel than claimed.** Temporal/Restate/BPM have done day-scale suspend/resume for years. The genuinely new part — correlating messy human replies — is also the most fragile part. If disambiguation-by-Resident becomes the common case, we built a clerk's inbox, not a primitive. (→ H1)
4. **The evidence loop is statistically anemic at personal scale.** ~10–50 tasks/week fragmented by (cause × task type × executor) gives n=1–3 cells; the ratchet has no statistical power; model swaps reset evidence. The gate verifies *occurrence*, not *quality* — "trust through evidence" is honestly "receipts plus a different LLM's opinion." (→ H2, H3)
5. **The Governor presumes stable root causes; LLM failures are often stochastic.** Confabulated causality hardened into policy; policies accumulate with no garbage collection. (→ H3, ossification guard)
6. **The single Resident is a bottleneck and an injection surface.** External content flows through worker reports into the judge's context; the gate checks evidence *existence*, not content *safety*. Prompt injection via a seller's reply is the most under-addressed threat. (open design item)
7. **The control group is cheap.** Claude Code + cron + a Telegram bot + a notes file covers much of the value if the bets fail. One operator maintaining a kernel is the homelab trap: the OS becomes the hobby, not the tool. (→ B7)
8. **Metaphor cosplay risk.** Every "OS-like" idea must answer *which of T1–T5 does this advance?* No answer → decoration.
9. **Self-serving definition risk.** We defined "Agent OS" in a way we are positioned to win. T4 (truth) being the category's missing duty is our belief, not proven market need. Guard: external validation signals count more than our own conviction.
10. **Role proliferation risk (new, 2026-07-03).** Resident + Governor + Jester + Voice for one operator is a lot of moving judgment. Each new role must earn its keep by its own bet below; a role whose bet fails gets deleted, not defended.
The qualification terms referenced here are fixed by the litmus-test definitions: [T1 third-party](agent-os-definition.md#t1), [T2 hostile program](agent-os-definition.md#t2), [T3 power loss](agent-os-definition.md#t3), [T4 liar](agent-os-definition.md#t4), and [T5 multiplexing](agent-os-definition.md#t5).

## 2. The original falsifiable bets (2026-06-11)

| Bet | Claim | Metric | Kill criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1 — correlation is real** | Replies from humans auto-match to waiting work on messy channels | Auto-match rate and Resident-disambiguation rate over 8 consecutive evaluation weeks, minimum 30 eligible replies | Auto-match **< 70%** (equivalently disambiguation **> 30%**) at the frozen evaluation boundary → `await world` downgraded from "primitive" to "assisted inbox" |
| **H2 — autonomy compounds** | Owner's *unplanned* interventions per completed task decline as evidence accumulates | Rescue rate in weeks 5–8 versus weeks 1–4 after #213+#215, minimum 40 completed tasks total and 10 in each half | Final-half rescue rate is not at least **10% relatively lower** than first-half rate → self-improvement narrative dropped; Governor frozen at reporting |
| **H3 — the Governor is net-positive** | Structural fixes prevent recurrence without ossifying | Mature fixes with zero recurrence divided by rolled-back fixes; throughput compares 4 weeks before with 8 evaluation weeks, minimum 12 mature fixes and 40 completed tasks in each throughput period | Ratio **< 3:1**, or median completed tasks/week falls **> 15%** without an external outage explanation → Governor demoted to weekly digest generator |

## 3. The philosophy-v2 bets (2026-07-03)

The new roles and framings from the v2 philosophy each carry their own kill switch.

| Bet | Claim | Metric | Kill criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B4 — the challenge is real** | An evidence-citing Resident objection produces corrections the Owner actually adopts | Adopted corrections from qualifying objections over 8 evaluation weeks, minimum 8 objections | **0 adopted** at the minimum sample → the "cognitive layer" framing is demoted; the Resident is redefined as a router with taste |
| **B5 — the Jester earns its seat** | Cheap real-time doubt catches real inconsistencies | For at least 20 mature adjudicated `jester.raised` challenges over 8 evaluation weeks, Governor precision is exactly `adopted/(adopted+dismissed)`; `answered_with_evidence` and `conceded` remain separate | Precision **< 60%** at the minimum sample, or the Owner keeps Jester muted for **14 consecutive days** after at least 5 challenges were delivered → demote or delete the role |
| **B6 — ingestion is earned per domain** | Always-on observation of a domain measurably improves judgment/challenge quality | Adopted proactive interventions from that domain over 8 evaluation weeks after promotion, minimum 10 interventions | **0 adopted** at the minimum sample → domain demoted back to on-demand |
| **B7 — the existence bet** | The kernel pays off only when stakes are real | Over 12 consecutive regular-use weeks and at least 60 completed delegated tasks: count false/overstated completion claims caught before admission and high-stakes delegations (money, external humans, or public output) | Fewer than **2** caught claims **and** fewer than **5** high-stakes delegations → adopt an off-the-shelf companion and retire the kernel ambition |
| **B8 — raw beats summary for the Governor** | Raw-trace diagnosis outperforms summary-fed diagnosis (the Meta-Harness ablation, reproduced on our incidents) | Blinded paired root-cause scoring on the same 20 incidents, with raw-first/summary-first order balanced 1:1 | Raw-access hit rate improves by **< 10 percentage points** over summary-only → drop the greppable-export kernel requirement and revisit storage costs |

Guards without full bets: **bypass erosion** triggers review when direct-to-Worker sessions exceed **25%** of eligible interactions in a rolling 8-week window with at least 40 interactions. **Goodhart on adoption** triggers recalibration when evaluator score and Owner adoption have absolute Spearman correlation **> 0.80** in two consecutive rolling 8-week windows, each with at least 30 adjudicated outcomes; semantic evaluation remains independent of adoption during recalibration.
### Frozen measurement and tuning procedure

These numbers are the conservative precommit as of 2026-07-25; earlier qualitative wording no longer defines a decision. An evaluation starts only after the named feature and its versioned telemetry are shipped. It then has a 14-day calibration period or the first 10 eligible observations, whichever is later. Calibration may adjust routing, prompts, and instrumentation, but not the thresholds, windows, samples, eligibility rules, or outcome labels above. Each adjustment is timestamped before evaluation. An external outage exclusion must be recorded within 24 hours of detection with provider/status evidence and frozen before boundary scoring. A material behavior/model change restarts the calibration and evaluation cohort rather than splicing data.
Eligibility is frozen as follows. An eligible H1 reply is a deduplicated non-test inbound response sent while one or more production waits are open. An eligible completed task is a non-test delegated WorkItem reaching terminal completion. A mature fix has been deployed for at least 4 weeks and observed across at least 10 subsequent affected-type tasks; rollback or recurrence adjudication then freezes its H3 label. A qualifying B4 objection cites durable evidence, reaches the Owner, and receives an `adopted` or `dismissed` adjudication. A mature B5 challenge has reached the Owner and received one of those two adjudications after its 7-day response window. A B6 intervention is proactive only when no Owner request prompted it and it receives the same adjudication. A B8 incident has a frozen Owner-adjudicated root cause before either blinded diagnosis is scored. An eligible bypass-guard interaction is a non-test Owner request that could be handled by the Resident or attached directly to a Worker. For B7, a regular-use week has at least 3 eligible completed tasks; test, replay, and calibration traffic is excluded everywhere.

At the frozen boundary, a bet with fewer than its minimum sample is `insufficient_data`, not a pass. Collection extends to at most 16 weeks (24 for B7); if still short, the project may make no positive claim for that bet and must publish the count and reason. Zero-denominator B5 precision is `insufficient_data`; for H3, zero rollbacks passes the ratio limb only when all 12 or more mature fixes have zero recurrence. Threshold changes require an Owner-approved prospective revision before a new cohort and never re-score an existing cohort. Rates use immutable eligible-event IDs; paired B8 cases and adjudication labels are frozen before either arm is scored.

## 4. Checkpoint cadence

Revision 9 is an Owner-approved clean break for an undeployed system. Checkpoint order is strict: checked manifest and complete producer census → #455 fresh-baseline one-ledger cutover → post-cutover census proving one writer and zero old production consumers → C1 → P3 (#456 package moves, #465 vocabulary/naming). The manifest/census gate being implemented does not qualify C1 or production wiring. #467 owns verifier/replay conformance, #469 stakes, #215 Wait/existing-agent messaging, and #459 the cross-issue roadmap.

- **C1 — `C1-restart-refute-reconcile-replay` (#455 integration gate)**: On a fresh P2 database, Resident appends WorkItem W before delivery; attempt A receives a unique ID/sequence, sends an awaited granted message to existing agent E with no allocation delta, and appends W's durable `Wait`. After process exit, restart folds the Wait, correlates E's reply, and resumes without replacement allocation. A's known-bad evidence is deterministically `refuted` with the checked predicate and W remains incomplete. Retry B receives a distinct ID/sequence with `retryOf=A`; A and B may share equivalence keys while both persist. B appends a workspace-bound, versioned-scope generic effect intent, performs one idempotent fake effect, crashes before confirmation, then restart reconciles that same intent and proves exactly one effect before accepted evidence permits completion. JSONL/sidecars export identity, Wait/reply, refutation, scope, intent/reconciliation, manifest inputs, and completion. Replay reproduces commands and the final fold from the recorded key/manifest, fails loudly on reducer drift or missing input, and performs zero live LLM, network, tool, or device calls. There is no legacy import/upcast assertion: the approved cutover has no compatibility path or dual writer.
- **C2 — #215 + #216 shipped**: preserve the pre-existing T1/T3 inspection boundary; H1 measurement begins.
- **C3 — measured regular use after C2**: apply the frozen protocol above (normally 8 weeks; B7 uses 12; sample-short cohorts may extend only to the stated cap), then append H2/H3 and any shipped B4–B8 verdicts with numerators, denominators, exclusions, and cohort versions.

## 5. Current verdict (pre-data)

- **As a design: A.** The axioms (separation of execution/judgment/improvement, work as the first-class unit, accountability as a kernel duty) target the category's unoccupied duties, and the v2 pass compressed the concept count instead of growing it.
- **As a running system: partial, not qualified.** Userland evidence-linked completion validation and supported read-back checks are wired on current paths, but they are not structural guarantees; the P2 C1 integration proof and role runtimes are not shipped. "Use OpenOmni as your Agent OS" cannot yet be honestly recommended.
- **Deciding variable: our own wiring conversion rate**, not competitors. The most probable failure path is the new specs becoming the next dormant engines. C1 is the test.

> The honest self-description until T1+T3+T4 pass: **"an evidence-gated personal workflow engine building toward an Agent OS."** The two product aims remain: *what you hand over gets finished; the same mistake doesn't happen twice.* They are goals measured by the bets above, not current guarantees.
